MOJO PHOTOGRAPHY - HOW TO ADD NEW PHOTOS

1. Resize the photo first: JPG, about 2500 px on the long side, under 3 MB.
   (Lightroom export, or the free website squoosh.app)
2. Give it a simple file name with no spaces, e.g.  leopard-tree.jpg
3. On GitHub, open this repository, click the photos folder,
   then Add file > Upload files. Drag the photo in and click "Commit changes".
4. Go back to the main page of the repository, open photos.js and click the pencil icon (Edit).
5. Copy the example line, paste it at the TOP of the PHOTOS list inside the [ ],
   and change file, genre, title, place and cam. Click "Commit changes".
6. Wait 1-2 minutes and refresh your website.

Videos: clips under 25 MB go in the videos folder. Anything longer, upload to YouTube
(Unlisted is fine) and paste the link into FILMS in photos.js.
